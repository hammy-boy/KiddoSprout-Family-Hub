# Language Garden content

Catalogue source: IANA Language Subtag Registry, https://www.iana.org/assignments/language-subtag-registry/language-subtag-registry, File-Date 2026-09-17. Includes 7,923 non-deprecated language subtags with individual/macrolanguage scopes. Collections, special values and private-use ranges are excluded. Names and alternate reference names are preserved. This catalogue is not a claim to cover every dialect, or to provide 7,923 courses.

Thirty starter packs, each with 18 word/phrase cards (540 total): Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Modern Standard Arabic, Japanese, Korean, English, Dutch, Swedish, Polish, Turkish, Swahili, Hindi, Mandarin Chinese (simplified), Russian, Greek, Yoruba, Indonesian, Romanian, Hungarian, Czech, Slovak, Finnish, Danish, Norwegian Bokmål, Ukrainian, Vietnamese and Esperanto. Added everyday replies, food/drink, animals, nature and learning words. These are authored introductory vocabulary samples, not fluent-speaker-reviewed curricula. Usage notes distinguish contextual yes/no answers, greetings, counting forms and varieties. Other catalogue entries remain explicitly marked unavailable; they do not borrow words from unrelated languages.

Yoruba greeting/phrase cross-checks: https://www.omniglot.com/language/phrases/yoruba.php and https://languageprofiles.ca/home/yoruba/ (accessed 2026-09-21). Swahili background reference: https://www.omniglot.com/writing/swahili.htm. These sources do not constitute a full review of all 540 cards. Fluent-speaker review is still welcome.

On-device speech is enabled only when the browser reports a matching local voice. No microphone, speech scoring, cloud translation, external audio service or sign-language video lessons are included. Custom text is never inserted as HTML. Custom cards, bookmarks and progress are local browser data, not cloud accounts. Existing Kiddo Sprout arcade approval applies.
