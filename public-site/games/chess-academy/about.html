A few things to know

Do I need an account?

No. Choose Play as guest to enjoy the academy. Account login is optional.

Where is my progress saved?

Puzzle progress, completed lessons, your notebook, and saved game history stay in this browser. They do not automatically follow you to another device.

How does the community work?

Shared community posts are not available on this GitHub Pages edition because it does not run a community server.

Is this connected to Chess.com or Lichess matchmaking?

No. This public edition does not offer online matchmaking. The Watch area separately offers Lichess TV and external broadcast links.
